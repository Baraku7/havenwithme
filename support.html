Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Haven → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. You'll keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for.

I already paid — how do I get my subscription back on a new device?

Open the Plans screen in Haven and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.

What happened to my free trial?

Each plan includes a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. If you cancel during the trial you won't be charged. Note that if you upgrade mid-trial, any unused portion of the trial is forfeited — that's Apple's policy, not ours.

Is my journal private?

Your journal entries are stored on your own device. Messages you send to the chat companion are transmitted securely to an AI service to generate a reply. Full detail is in our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email us at baraku7@gmail.com from the address on your account and we'll delete your account information within a reasonable time. Anything stored only on your device is removed when you delete the app.

The chat isn't responding

Check your internet connection first, then close and reopen the app. If it persists, email us with a rough time it happened so we can look at it.

Is Haven a replacement for therapy?

No. Haven is a self-help and wellbeing app. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and is not a substitute for care from a qualified professional. If you'd like human support, the Therapists section can help you find licensed professionals near you.
